The **PowerHub XT90** is a distribution board for up to 5 XT90 connectors, inspired by the [PowerHub XT60](./Powerhub_XT60), but modified quite a bit.

## Parts references
### Fuse holder
The fuse hodler ist designed to work with 5x20 fuses as well as standard car fuses.
* 5x20 Fuse holder: [Littlefuse 445](https://www.reichelt.de/de/de/shop/produkt/sicherungshalter_fuer_5_x_20_mm_10_a-242453) - up to 10A
* car fuse holder: Keystone 3522 - up to 30A
  * [full holder with two connectors](https://www.reichelt.de/de/de/shop/produkt/sicherungshalter_ato_atc_30_a_500_v_2-fach_print-229146)
  * [individual connectors](https://www.reichelt.de/de/de/shop/produkt/sicherungshalter_ato_atc_30_a_500_v_1-fach_print-229145)

### Non-XT90 connectores
6.35mm spade connectors
* [straight spade connector](https://www.reichelt.de/de/de/shop/produkt/flachstecker_fuer_printmontage_breite_6_35mm-231353)
* [angled spade connector](https://www.reichelt.de/de/de/shop/produkt/flachstecker_fuer_printmontage_breite_6_35mm-231355)

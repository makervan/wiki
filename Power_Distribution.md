### Tindie Products
None of them are open source, but interesting as references nonetheless

* [DC power hub](https://www.tindie.com/products/untemed/dc-power-hub/)
  * Super simple, input via barrel jack, output via barrel jacks with fuses
* [USB control hub with power distribution board](https://www.tindie.com/products/sp3dtech/usb-control-hub-with-power-distribution-board/)
  * 12V outputs, 5V USB outputs and USB hub, with automotive fuses. Kinf of expensive
* [5 .. 12 Volt DC Distribution Board 5.5x2.1mm plugs](https://www.tindie.com/products/robbedoes/5-12-volt-dc-distribution-board-55x21mm-plugs/)
  * Again super simple, input via barrel jack, output via barrel jacks with automotive fuses
* [deluxe dc distribution board IOT](https://www.tindie.com/products/robbedoes/deluxe-dc-distribution-board-iot/)
  * Adds usb ports to the previous board
  * Power measurement on all ports
* [Deluxe IOT 12/5V measured & fused distribution pcb](https://www.tindie.com/products/robbedoes/deluxe-iot-125v-measured-fused-distribution-pcb/)
